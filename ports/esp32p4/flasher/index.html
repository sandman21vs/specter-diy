Specter-DIY Web Flasher

Flash Specter-DIY to the Waveshare ESP32-P4 4.3" board from your browser

⚠ Your browser does not support the Web Serial API. Please use Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Brave on a desktop computer.
⚠ Development builds. Do not use with real funds. No secure boot and no flash encryption: anyone with physical access to the board can read or replace what is on it. Flashing also wipes everything stored on the device. Use it to test and play, on testnet.
How to flash
  1. Plug the board into its UART USB-C port with a data cable.
  2. Pick the firmware below (the latest release is selected).
  3. Click Connect, choose the board's serial port, then Flash.
  4. The board reboots and shows the Specter screen. If Connect fails, hold BOOT, tap RST, release BOOT and try again.
